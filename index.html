01 — Before you begin

How to use the 1–9 scale

For each pair of criteria below, judge how much more important one is than the other for prioritising decentralized storage techniques. Choose 1 if they matter equally; move toward 9 the more strongly you favour one side.

ValueMeaning
1Equal importance — both criteria contribute the same to the prioritisation decision.
3Moderate importance — experience or judgement slightly favours one criterion over the other.
5Strong importance — one criterion is strongly favoured over the other.
7Very strong importance — one criterion's dominance is demonstrated in practice.
9Extreme importance — the evidence favouring one criterion is as strong as possible.
2, 4, 6, 8 are intermediate values for when your judgement sits between two of the levels above. You never need to enter a fraction: sliding toward the left criterion or the right criterion automatically records the correct reciprocal (e.g. favouring the right side at strength 5 is stored as 1/5 in the left criterion's row of the comparison matrix).
